# Preserve and synthesize contributions

Represent an atomic contribution by the problem it solves, mechanism, evidence, operating conditions, exceptions, minute difference from neighbors, and a distinguishing test. Split compound claims when they require different evidence or conditions.

Compare overlapping ideas across behavior, state, failure recovery, timing, appearance, cost, and dependencies. “Both preserve drafts” can hide memory-only versus durable storage, local versus server recovery, or unconditional versus version-aware restoration. Group for retrieval without deleting those differences.

Keep the original before summarizing. A merge must retain conditions, exceptions, contradictory evidence, and source links. If synthesis fails or its completeness is uncertain, retain all inputs and mark the merged view provisional. Test retrieval after context reset: can a worker recover the small difference and its original evidence?

Use scoped, reversible statuses:
- Candidate: relevant, insufficiently qualified.
- Adopted: selected for stated conditions and purpose.
- Challenger: plausible alternative worth comparing.
- Deferred: retained, with a reopening trigger.
- Contradicted: evidence conflicts with a scoped claim; retain the claim and counterevidence.

Popularity, overlap, age, or no current need are not sufficient grounds for deletion. A hard conflict or demonstrated harm can exclude something from the current composition; record why and what could reverse it. Scope incompatibility to the actual implementation and conditions: persistence without cleanup can conflict with session privacy, while a different lifecycle may fit. Do not mark the entire mechanism false merely because its supplied variant is unsuitable. True equivalence requires the decision-relevant conditions to match.

Spend deeply on decision-changing uncertainty while keeping broad inexpensive retention. Avoid fabricated precise scores. Preserve specialist branches where a universal compromise would lose strength. Visual references should resolve into a coherent language; mechanical feature accumulation does not create coherence.

For each consequential adoption, create a transfer contract:
- Objective and conditions.
- Reference property and recoverable evidence.
- Invariant to preserve and intentional adaptation.
- Dependencies and likely interference with other contributions.
- Discriminating check and reversal trigger.

For expert-process contributions, preserve situation cues, exceptions, evidence-seeking actions, and completion criteria in the actual assignment or retrieved guidance. Decide the carrier separately from the contribution: prompt, reference, skill, executable tool, or feedback workflow. Use [expert-process transfer](expert-processes.md) for that decision and its checks.

Compare original, adapted, and combined mechanisms on representative cases when practical. Test strategically important interactions and challenger substitutions; exhaustive powersets are rarely feasible. A combination is an improvement hypothesis until evidence supports it.

Trace contracts into actual files, outputs, and complete journeys. If a correction changes the objective or operating conditions, reopen affected decisions and checks, including dependencies. Keep inactive useful ideas accessible rather than silently forgetting them at handoff.
